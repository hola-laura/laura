02 / About

I've spent the last several years shaping how fintech products work at the systems level — defining frameworks that govern cross-team experiences, building design infrastructure that outlasts any single feature, and making complex multi-partner integrations feel effortless to customers. I think about what happens between the screens, not just on them. The lifecycle, the routing, the transitions, the endings.

Before Monzo, I led design for healthcare safety software used across 5,000+ hospitals. I move between consumer and enterprise, mobile and web, but the constant is the same: I design the rules, not just the surfaces.
