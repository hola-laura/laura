01 / The challenge

Hundreds of ways to find one answer.

Just Eaters had hundreds of unconnected ways to reach news, knowledge and information at work.

Finding anything took time, and people struggled to connect with colleagues around the world.

02 / Research

Ask people where they already read.

The internal newsletter had a high open rate, so we embedded a six-question survey in it.

Alongside employee interviews, it pointed to five problems, from finding toolkits to getting answers about salaries and holidays.

03 / The workplace

Productive, joined-up and engaging.

Stay in the know

Top news, daily

The latest stories from across Just Eat, away from the inbox.

Start a conversation

Communities in one place

Groups for projects, events and fun, with comments, likes and polls on every news article.

Get more done

Tools at a glance

One Google login, global search, and mail, calendar and files on the homepage, on any device.

04 / The result

A homepage for the whole company.

The homepage brings company news, personal tools and communities together. Department pages and a blog make it easier to see what other teams are doing.
