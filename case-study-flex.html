01 / Reframe the promise

The product said “pay later”. Customers already used credit cards.

Flex was 15% behind its acquisition plan. A survey of 1,924 eligible customers showed why: 65% used credit cards, while only 9% used BNPL.

So we repositioned Flex as an everyday credit card, with clear pricing and no hidden fees.

The new framing lifted account creation by 7.1%. In a separate test, awareness rose by 20%.

02 / Bring the account together

One balance. A clearer starting point.

37% of customers had more than ten active purchases. Managing them one by one made the account hard to scan.

I brought the card, balance, limit, next payments and activity into one view.

9/10participants completed tasks unaided in the second usability round.
03 / Make repayments understandable

Show what each choice changes.

An extra payment doesn’t always lower the next one. The flow now explains that before customers confirm.

The remaining balance and interest saved stay visible, so every choice is clear.

Phased after the first release

Change this month. See the months ahead.

Editing a monthly amount was complex, so we shipped the clearer account first. Then we added a view of how a change affects future payments and interest.
