The brief

We started with a question, not a feature.

Since Monzo launched its gambling block, customers had asked to block other places too. How could we help them interrupt unwanted spending, while keeping the choice in their hands?

01 / Find the focus

Start with the people. Then decide what to build.

I ran lightning talks and a workshop with vulnerability specialists, designers and researchers.

  1. Sources
  2. Facts
  3. Insights
  4. Opportunities
  5. A shared focus
02 / Give them the choice

Their boundary. Their decision.

They pick the merchant. A reason is optional. We never say a purchase was wrong.

03 / A pause in their own words

A reminder from present you to future you.

Not a warning from the bank. Write your reminder, then try to unblock.

  • Unblocking is always possible.
  • It just starts with what you told yourself.
04 / The calls

Four decisions that shaped the release.

  1. 01

    Support, without judgement.

    Situation
    A reason helps us understand. But some customers worried a block could be read as a sign they were struggling.
    Call
    Reasons are optional, with “I’d rather not share” as an answer. We never say a purchase was wrong.
    Result
    Customers set the boundary on their own terms.
  2. 02

    Their words, not ours.

    Situation
    Unblocking had to stay possible, but not thoughtless.
    Call
    Before unblocking, show the reminder they wrote, instead of a warning from the bank.
    Result
    A moment to reconsider. The choice stays theirs.
  3. 03

    Merchants, not categories.

    Situation
    Category blocks sounded more powerful. But the gambling block had shown that merchants who label themselves differently slip through.
    Call
    Block specific merchants, on card payments. Bank transfers needed support-team help, so they waited.
    Result
    Reliable enough to ship in 2 weeks with one engineer. 10,000+ customers adopted it.
  4. 04

    Left for later, on purpose.

    Not yet
    Time-based blocks and a delay before unblocking.
    Honest note
    Adoption shows people wanted it. It doesn’t show reduced financial harm.
    Next time
    Measure whether blocks hold: how often people keep the block after reading their reminder.
