01 / Start with the buying experience

The mortgage is
only part of the job.

Earlier research with 877 aspiring and recent buyers pointed to needs beyond finding a deal: knowing what happens next, understanding property issues and coordinating the people involved.

The 2026 pilot proposal explored how Monzo could bring more of that support together.

02 / Three ways to help

Be clear about what we are offering.

A / Mortgage advice

Find a deal.

Fee-free mortgage advice, with more of the experience inside Monzo.

B / Coordinated service

Bring it together.

A paid service connecting mortgage advice, legal work and a survey.

C / Guidance

Know what comes next.

Free guidance and visibility of the milestones on the way to a new home.

These routes make different promises. The pilot needs to show which promise customers understand and value, before we invest in a broader service.

03 / What the pilot needs to establish

Make the next decision easier.

01 / Relevance

Are we reaching buyers?

02 / Understanding

Is the promise clear?

Check what customers expect each service to include and what they think it costs.

03 / Value

Does it help them move forward?

Measure useful progress and take-up, alongside confusion and support needs.
