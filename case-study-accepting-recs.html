Monzo · Mortgage recommendations · 2026

A big decision.
A clear next step.

Helping customers understand a mortgage recommendation, what accepting it means and what happens afterwards.

Design exploration · 2026
The design challenge

Confidence before commitment.

Show the recommendation. Explain the choice. Make the handoff clear.

This study focuses on the design exploration. Launch outcomes are not included.

01 / Understand02 / Decide03 / Continue
01 / Make the recommendation readable

Help people see
what they are choosing.

A mortgage recommendation carries a lot of information. The design brings the options together, with a route into the detail when customers need it.

The aim is a decision people understand, with space to ask for advice.

02 / Explain the commitment

A clear action.
No hidden next step.

The confirmation step needs to say what accepting a recommendation does, and what still needs to happen.

That clarity matters more than making the button easy to tap.

03 / Keep the journey connected

The decision is
not the end.

After a decision, customers need to know who will help, what is happening and whether they need to do anything.

These designs connect the recommendation with the partner handoff and progress tracker. The next evidence to collect would be comprehension, wrong turns and useful progress—not acceptance alone.
