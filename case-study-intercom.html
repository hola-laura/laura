01 / Make it clear02 / Route with care03 / Test the risk
01 / Start with identity

“Who am I talking to?”
Make the answer obvious.

Habito advisers used Intercom. Monzo Support used a different system. The design needed to explain that boundary without making customers learn how the systems worked.

Partner identity, clear entry points and a way back to the right conversation became the core of the journey.

02 / Design the recovery, too

A wrong turn should be easy to fix.

An active remortgage

Mortgage advice

Take customers to the partner adviser who can help with their case.

A banking question

Monzo Support

Keep account questions with the team that can answer them.

An unclear question

Ask, then guide

Clarify what someone needs and offer a direct route if they are in the wrong chat.

Entry into partner chat depended on the customer having an active remortgage. Deep links, Help and unread-message states all needed to carry that context.

03 / Plan how to learn safely

A pilot with
clear stop points.

The pilot plan set thresholds for messages sent to the wrong team: investigate at 2%, revert at 5%.

Those were operating thresholds, not observed error rates. The next proof would be whether customers found the right help and recovered easily from a wrong turn.

Search behaviour, deep links and notifications were dependencies to resolve before treating this as a finished experience.
